🔎 How we chose

We use the cumulative rating and total review count from Agoda's search response, the fetched nightly price, and transit distance. Ratings receive a Bayesian adjustment for review volume and combine with the price tier; traveler-type fit is computed only from the snippet sample in the response. Missing prices, amenities and locations are not estimated. This ranking reflects the conditions above — re-check live prices and availability at checkout.
