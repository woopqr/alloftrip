About All of Trip

All of Trip is a data-backed guide to the world's finest stays. Our aim isn't to list every hotel — it's to make the reason behind each pick clear, so you can choose a truly special place with confidence.

What we compare

Under the same search conditions, we compare each property's cumulative rating, total review count, nightly price, distance to key landmarks and verifiable amenities. We focus on the top end of the market — five-star hotels, luxury resorts and private pool villas — and organize the results so honeymooners, couples celebrating an anniversary, golf travelers and wellness seekers can narrow their options quickly.

How we present the data

We distinguish the total review count from the smaller review sample included in a search response. Statistics drawn from a small sample are marked as directional only, and we never invent prices, amenities or locations that a source doesn't confirm. Every article shows the time of fetch, the search criteria, the data source and the known limits.

Price observation

We periodically observe nightly prices across cities under identical search conditions. These observations are not confirmed booking totals, and taxes, member rates and availability can change them. See the price observatory.

Editing & corrections

Content is published under the All of Trip data desk. We review error reports against the evidence and update the underlying data and documents accordingly. Our ranking math is described in Methodology, and our affiliate and editorial principles in the Editorial policy.
