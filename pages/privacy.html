2. Cookies & Google AdSense

The site displays ads through Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve personalized ads based on your visit history.

  • Google uses advertising cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites.

The site contains links to external sites such as Agoda. Those sites' own privacy policies govern how they handle your data, and we are not responsible for them.
